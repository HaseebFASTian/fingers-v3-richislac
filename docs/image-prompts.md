# Richislac image specifications

Created with the built-in Image Gen tool. The product artwork supplied with the approved catalogue was used as the bottle reference. Generated lifestyle scenes are illustrative brand visuals, not photographs of the company's facilities or customers.

Final assets are in `public/assets/`. PNG generation masters remain in the local generated-images directory; the website uses optimized WebP exports. Product cards retain the existing catalogue-based artwork rather than inventing additional products.

## Hero bottle

File: `public/assets/hero-milk-bottle.webp` — 800 × 1200, transparent.

Prompt specification: Create a premium photorealistic studio product cutout of the supplied Richislac 1 L full cream milk bottle. Preserve the handled white bottle, blue screw cap, Richislac label, colours and proportions. Show a clean three-quarter front view with readable branding, subtle condensation, natural plastic texture and dimensional studio light. Transparent background. One bottle only; no extra objects, captions or invented label claims.

## Milk splash

File: `public/assets/milk-splash.webp` — 1536 × 1024, transparent.

Prompt specification: A high-speed studio photograph of a satin-white milk splash forming a broad U-shaped crown, with curved upward arcs and suspended droplets. Keep the centre open for a separately layered milk bottle. Soft blue highlights, realistic thickness and smooth liquid detail. Transparent background. No bottle, fruit, text or other objects.

## Breakfast moment

File: `public/assets/breakfast-moment.webp` — 1536 × 1024.

Prompt specification: A naturally lit commercial breakfast photograph featuring the supplied Richislac 1 L milk bottle on the right of a warm cream table. Preserve the exact bottle branding and shape. Include a breakfast cereal bowl, glass of milk, toast, banana and a blue cloth. Leave a quiet pale wall on the left. Sunny window light, realistic shadows and an inviting everyday setting. No people, slogans or additional product claims.

## Shared goodness

## Everyday breakfast background

File: `public/assets/everyday-breakfast-background.webp` — 1774 × 887. Created with the built-in Image Gen tool, exported to WebP at quality 86. The scene is static; no parallax, video or background animation is applied.

Prompt specification: Create a wide, approximately 2:1, photorealistic commercial dairy breakfast scene in an airy sunlit kitchen. Keep the left 60 percent calm with a warm ivory plaster wall, ivory stone table and soft morning shadows, leaving space for dark navy website copy. Arrange a clear glass jug of milk, glass of milk, cream ceramic cereal bowl and golden toast on a plate in the middle-right. Add muted blue linen, delicate sage greenery and a softly blurred garden window. Use a table-height three-quarter camera angle, realistic texture, dimensional natural light and subtle glass reflections. Keep all objects in frame. Palette: ivory, milk white, soft sky blue, tiny natural green and golden toast accents. No people, text, typography, logos, branded packaging, watermarks, pink or purple. Render only the photographic scene, not a website mockup.
