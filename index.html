A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

LET'S CLEAR
THINGS UP.

Products, packs and how to get in touch.

What is in the Richislac range?

Our current range includes Full Cream Milk in a 1 L bottle and 1 L gable-top carton, plus a 250 ml Whey Protein Drink.

Is the whey protein drink a milk bottle?

No. It is a separate product in a 250 ml can. Our full cream milk is available in the 1 L bottle and gable-top carton.

Where can I find ingredients and storage details?

Please refer to your product's label for ingredients, allergens, storage instructions and shelf life. Include the exact product in your enquiry if you need more information.

Can I enquire for my shop or business?

Yes. Save the products you are interested in, then prepare an enquiry with your business name, city and requirements.

Can I order or pay online?

This website showcases our products. Online orders and payments are not available. The enquiry form prepares a local preview you can download; it does not send a message.
